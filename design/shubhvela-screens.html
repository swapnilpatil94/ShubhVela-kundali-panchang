ShubhVela · final screens v1.0 · Ratna Glass design system

All 28 screens as decided. Navigation is a floating glass dock with a live Aaj orb at its centre: the ring shows how much of the current choghadiya is left, and the diya is lit when now is shubh and turns kumkum when it isn't. The active tab glows in the day's gem colour. Content sits on solid lacquer. Liquid glass is used only for the tab bar, sheets and floating controls. Gold means auspicious or primary, and kumkum means avoid. The day's gemstone (Vaar Ratna) tints the sky header, with the live hora planet in it and a text-safe zone behind all text (≥7:1). The Aaj screen reads in three layers: the Abhi answer, then the Surya Path, then the Choghadiya Patti. The Kaal Chakra appears only on the watch, widgets and Pro view. Sacred screens are immersive and have no ads. Custom line icons; no emoji or Om in the chrome. Sample data: Pune, Saturday 15 Nov 2026, 8:52 AM (Kartik Shukla Shashthi, Chhath Puja). Values come from an approximate calculation; the engine must confirm them. Deity art and real NASA/ESA sky images will replace the placeholders and generated stand-ins.
