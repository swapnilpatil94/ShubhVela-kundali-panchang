# M10 Kundli and family
**Goal:** on-device profiles (CRUD, encrypted) with birth chart, planets, Vimshottari to sookshma, yogas/doshas, gochar/Sade Sati, and family dates (tithi/nakshatra birthdays, shraddha) with reminders.
**Implements:** MASTER Part 12 M10 (l.1422); Part 2 §D D1, D2, D3, D5, D9, D11; Part 3 §5, §7 "Family dates", §8, §9 (overlay, noon chart), §10; Part 5 Data storage; DESIGN_V2 §8 `KundliChart`, `FamilyAvatars`, `FamilyDateRow`, screens 13 and 16.

## Scope decisions (log in docs/DECISIONS.md under "M10")
- **D1** "D2, D3, D5 and D9" in the M10 entry are the **Part 2 §D feature IDs**, not vargas (D5 is not a Shodashvarga division). Divisional charts D2–D60 are D4, which is R2. Chart chips are **D1 and D9 only**: §9 hides charts "above D9", so D9 belongs to R1. The Chalit and KP chips shown in PNG 13 are R2 and stay hidden.
- **D2** Dasha depth is 4 (maha > antar > pratyantar > sookshma), per D3.
- **D3** Shraddha is in scope (D11 and PNG 16). A profile gains an optional date of passing. Payload version 1 becomes 2 through `PayloadMigrations`: v1 rows read `passing = null` and `familyPrefs = default`.
- **D4** Yearly family dates are evaluated at the **active home place and zone**, because reminders fire where the user lives. Janma tithi, nakshatra and solar rashi at a known birth instant are geocentric, so they do not depend on the place.
- **D5** A profile whose stored ayanamsa is disabled falls back to Lahiri and shows a visible note. It never crashes (M07 decision).
- **D6** No new library, permission, network call or ads. "Share card / remind siblings" is M12 and is hidden.

## Rules (no judgement calls)
1. **Birth instant.** `localToInstant(LocalDateTime(date, time), place.tzContext(), ambiguity, manual)`. `birthInstant == null` exactly when the time is unknown. If the flag is NONEXISTENT_DATE or GAP_SHIFTED, the editor blocks Save and shows "this clock time did not exist; check the time or set an offset". If AMBIGUOUS, show both offsets as a two-option picker (EARLIER default, LATER). The editor always shows the offset and its source (IANA/OVERLAY/LMT/MANUAL). HISTORICAL_VERIFY shows note HISTORICAL_OFFSET; PRE_STANDARD_LMT shows LMT_OFFSET. A manual offset goes through `OffsetPickerModel` (range −12:00..+14:00).
2. **Unknown time (noon chart, §9).** Compute at local 12:00 at the birth place. Hide the lagna, houses, house numbers and the lagna marker. Disable NORTH style, which is house-based, and offer SOUTH/EAST, which are sign-fixed. Show D9 without its lagna. The dasha shows a "birth time unknown: approximate" note. Family janma facts come from `janmaOfDay`: if one tithi and one nakshatra prevail over the whole civil day (local midnight to midnight), the result is `Exact`; otherwise `Ambiguous`, and the user picks the tithi and nakshatra before family dates are generated.
3. **Charts.** `ChartCalculator.compute(instant, place, calc)`; D9 is `vargas.chart(Varga.D9, facts, calc)`. D1 houses are WHOLE_SIGN for display (§5). Cell house h holds rashi (lagna + h − 1) mod 12.
   - **NORTH**: square with both diagonals and the midpoint diamond. House 1 is the top-centre diamond; houses run counter-clockwise (2 top-left upper triangle … 4 left diamond, 7 bottom diamond, 10 right diamond, 12 top-right upper triangle). Each cell is labelled with its rashi number 1..12 (PNG 13).
   - **SOUTH**: 4×4 outer ring, signs fixed. Top row Mi Me Vr Mt (Pi Ar Ta Ge); right column Kk, Si; bottom row right-to-left Kn Tu Vs Dh; left column bottom-up Mk, Ku. The lagna cell gets a diagonal stroke.
   - **EAST**: 3×3 grid with diagonally split corners, Aries top-centre, signs counter-clockwise. **VERIFY** the exact cell order against an owner-supplied reference image (`docs/fixtures/east-chart.png`, TODO(owner)).
   - Glyphs are two-letter codes in gem colours from string resources; ‘R’ suffix when retrograde.
4. **Planets tab.** For each graha and the lagna: rashi, degInRashi as D°MM′, nakshatra name, pada, nakshatra lord (lords cycle Ke Ve Su Mo Ma Ra Ju Sa Me from Ashwini), retrograde flag. Avakhada comes from `NakshatraTable.avakhada`.
5. **Dasha.** `Dashas.vimshottari(facts.of(MOON).lon, instant, calc year length)`. The current chain is `tree.at(now, 4)`; current rows are highlighted and auto-expanded. Dates display in the home zone, date-only; boundaries come straight from the engine and are never rounded before comparison. Header line, as in PNG 13: "Guru › Budh › Shukra · antardasha till <date>".
6. **Yogas/doshas (D5).** `YogaRules.evaluate(facts)`: show the name (string resource keyed by rule id), present/absent, the variant used (Manglik), and RuleStatus VERIFY as "under review". No descriptions (M11) and no remedies.
7. **Gochar (D9).** `Gochar.snapshot(natal, chart(now))` and `sadeSati(natal Moon rashi, transit Saturn rashi)`. The Sade Sati timeline is the Saturn ingresses from `Gochar.ingresses(SATURN, now − 3 y, now + 10 y)` mapped through `sadeSati`.
8. **Family date defs** (Part 3 §7). Every default below is **VERIFY** (TODO(owner), pandit) and lives in one constant table `FamilyDateDefaults`:

| Kind | Rule built | Defaults (VERIFY) |
|---|---|---|
| TITHI_BIRTHDAY | `TithiAt([facts.tithi], label(system).masa, system, kala, tie, null, none)` | kala SUNRISE, tie FIRST, none CONTAINING_START, adhika NIJA; born in an adhika masa → kept in the nija month of the same name |
| NAKSHATRA_BIRTHDAY SOLAR | `NakshatraInSolarMonth(facts.nakshatra, facts.sunRashi, region, SUNRISE, pick)` | pick FIRST; no sunrise day in the month → no occurrence, show "no matching day this year" (no guessed fallback) |
| NAKSHATRA_BIRTHDAY LUNAR | `NakshatraInLunarMonth(facts.nakshatra, label(system).masa, system, SUNRISE, pick)` | pick FIRST, adhika NIJA; none → as above |
| SHRADDHA | `TithiAt([deathTithi], label(system).masa, system, APARAHNA, tie, null, none)` | deathTithi = tithi at the passing instant (time known, home zone) or at sunrise of the passing date at the home place (time unknown); tie FIRST, none CONTAINING_START |

   `NakshatraInLunarMonth`: the days of the lunation(s) named `masa` (per `system`, adhika policy applied) whose `kala` the nakshatra touches, then `pick`. It reuses the `NakshatraInSolarMonth` touch test.
9. **Reminders.** `ProfileFamilyDateSource.dates(first, last)` returns the dates of every enabled `familyPrefs` kind for every profile. It returns bare dates only; `ReminderContent.FamilyDate` keeps no fields. `ReminderPlanner` is unchanged: 19:00 the evening before, at most 2 per day, priority unchanged, and it runs only if `ReminderKind.FAMILY_DATES` is opted in.
10. **Threading and cache.** ViewModels call `KundliEngine` on `Dispatchers.Default`; the app delegate takes the `EngineAccess` lock. In-memory LRU of 8 `KundliBundle`s keyed by `(profileId, updatedAt, calc.hashCode, noon, homePlaceId)`. Never on disk. Evicted on profile delete and on vault lock.
