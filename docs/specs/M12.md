# M12 Bonds and sharing: spec
**Goal:** offline, on-device compatibility between two Moon points (Ashtakoota 36 + Manglik note), a private versioned Bond Code, a Bonds tab, identity and bond cards shared through system intents. No server, no account, no birth data leaves the device.
**Implements:** MASTER Part 12 M12; Part 2 §F (F1 North, F2, F3, F6, F9 free theme); Part 3 §5 (Manglik); Part 5 (STORAGE, PLATFORM deep links, input validation, under-18); Part 7 (no ads yet); Part 8 (all); DESIGN_V2 §8 `BondOrbit`, `IdentityCard`, `ShareCardFrame`; screens 18, 19, 20.

## Scope cuts (log each in docs/DECISIONS.md)
- D1 South Dashakoota/10 Porutham, Rajju, Vedha moved to **M12b**: tables need a pandit fixture first (F1 South is still owed).
- D2 No QR: there is no QR library in the version catalog. Share as text, a link or the share sheet only.
- D3 Play Install Referrer and the referral reward move to M15 (the library is not cached, and the reward is ad-free days). Instagram Stories needs the owner's Facebook App ID, so it is TODO(owner) and M15.
- D4 F9: only the free "Minimal" theme ships. The R/P themes need M15 rewarded/billing.
- D5 Bond Code adds `vs` (Vashya class, 3 bits). It drops the cached `g/y/nd` and recomputes them from `mn`. Update the Part 8 schema the same way.
- D6 F3 bond types score **symmetrically**: the mean of (A as groom, B as bride) and (B as groom, A as bride). F1 marriage matching asks for the bride and groom roles explicitly.

## Rules
Indices: nakshatra 0 = Ashwini … 26 = Revati. Rashi 0 = Mesha … 11 = Meena. Gana, yoni and nadi come from `rules/nakshatra.json`. Every table below is VERIFY: TODO(owner), pandit sign-off plus a 20-pair fixture `fixtures/koota_pandit.csv`.
1. **Varna (4Q).** Ranks: Brahmin 4 = {3,7,11}, Kshatriya 3 = {0,4,8}, Vaishya 2 = {1,5,9}, Shudra 1 = {2,6,10}. Score 4Q if rank(groom) ≥ rank(bride), else 0.
2. **Vashya (8Q).** Classes by sign: 0 Ch, 1 Ch, 2 Ma, 3 Ja, 4 Va, 5 Ma, 6 Ma, 7 Ke, 8: lon < 15° Ma else Ch, 9: lon < 15° Ch else Ja, 10 Ma, 11 Ja. Exactly 15.0° counts as the second half. Points matrix (rows groom, cols bride, order Ch Ma Ja Va Ke), **LOW confidence, VERIFY**: Ch [2,1,1,0.5,1]; Ma [1,2,0.5,0,1]; Ja [1,0.5,2,1,1]; Va [0.5,0,1,2,0]; Ke [1,1,1,0,2].
3. **Tara (12Q).** c = ((nX − nY) mod 27) + 1 and t = ((c − 1) mod 9) + 1. Bad if t ∈ {3,5,7}. Evaluate X = groom, Y = bride, and the reverse. Both good: 12Q; one good: 6Q; neither: 0.
4. **Yoni (16Q).** Order: Horse, Elephant, Sheep, Serpent, Dog, Cat, Rat, Cow, Buffalo, Tiger, Deer, Monkey, Mongoose, Lion. Symmetric matrix, VERIFY:
   H 4 2 2 3 2 2 2 1 0 1 3 3 2 1 | E 2 4 3 3 2 2 2 2 3 1 2 3 2 0 | Sh 2 3 4 2 1 2 1 3 3 1 2 0 3 1 | Se 3 3 2 4 2 1 1 1 1 2 2 2 0 2 | D 2 2 1 2 4 2 1 2 2 1 0 2 1 1 | C 2 2 2 1 2 4 0 2 2 1 3 3 2 1 | R 2 2 1 1 1 0 4 2 2 2 2 2 1 2 | Co 1 2 3 1 2 2 2 4 3 0 3 2 2 1 | B 0 3 3 1 2 2 2 3 4 1 2 2 2 1 | T 1 1 1 2 1 1 2 0 1 4 1 1 2 1 | De 3 2 2 2 0 3 2 3 2 1 4 2 2 1 | M 3 3 0 2 2 3 2 2 2 1 2 4 3 2 | Mo 2 2 3 0 1 2 1 2 2 2 2 3 4 2 | L 1 0 1 2 1 1 2 1 1 1 1 2 2 4.
   Points × 4 = Q. The seven 0-pairs are the sworn enemies. `KootaTables.parse` rejects the matrix if it is not symmetric.
5. **Graha Maitri (20Q).** Sign lords: Ma Ve Me Mo Su Me Ve Ma Ju Sa Sa Ju. Natural relations (friend / neutral / enemy): Su F{Mo,Ma,Ju} N{Me} E{Ve,Sa}; Mo F{Su,Me} N{Ma,Ju,Ve,Sa}; Ma F{Su,Mo,Ju} N{Ve,Sa} E{Me}; Me F{Su,Ve} N{Ma,Ju,Sa} E{Mo}; Ju F{Su,Mo,Ma} N{Sa} E{Me,Ve}; Ve F{Me,Sa} N{Ma,Ju} E{Su,Mo}; Sa F{Me,Ve} N{Ju} E{Su,Mo,Ma}. Points by the pair of views: same lord or F-F 5, F-N 4, N-N 3, F-E 1, N-E 0.5, E-E 0. Reuse an existing engine friendship table if there is one, and assert it equals this one.
6. **Gana (24Q).** Rows groom, cols bride, order Deva Manushya Rakshasa, VERIFY: D [6,6,1]; M [5,6,0]; R [1,0,6].
7. **Bhakoot (28Q).** d1 = ((rG − rB) mod 12) + 1 and d2 = ((rB − rG) mod 12) + 1. If {d1,d2} ∈ {{2,12},{5,9},{6,8}}, score 0 and add note `koota.bhakoot.dosha`. Otherwise 28Q.
8. **Nadi (32Q).** Same nadi: 0, plus note `koota.nadi.same`. Otherwise 32Q.
9. **Exceptions.** The Nadi exceptions (same rashi with a different nakshatra, or the same nakshatra with a different pada) and the Bhakoot exceptions (same or friendly lords) are **notes only** (`koota.*.exception_possible`, VERIFY) and never change Q. The UI states "Base rule; exceptions not applied."
10. **Total and percent.** Total = ΣQ (max 144). For BESTIE and SIBLING, exclude BHAKOOT and NADI (max 84) and show `bond.friend_excludes_note`. percent = (200·Q + max) div (2·max), integer half-up. Whether PARENT, WORK and ROOMMATE also exclude them is VERIFY(owner); until then they use 144.
11. **Labels and axes.** Labels come from `bond_weights.json` bands. Provisional bands, TODO(owner): ≥80 Cosmic Twins, 65–79 Soul Squad, 50–64 Spicy Combo, 35–49 Opposites Attract, <35 Growth Bond. Never a negative label. Never "pass/fail" and no 18-guna threshold wording (VERIFY wording with the pandit). axis = half-up(10·Σwᵢqᵢ / Σwᵢ·maxQᵢ), with integer wᵢ ≥ 0. Provisional weights for every type: Vibe {Gana, Yoni}, Talk {GrahaMaitri, Varna}, Trust {Tara, Vashya}, Fights {Bhakoot, Gana}, Growth {Nadi, Tara}. For friend types, Bhakoot and Nadi weights must be 0 (Fights {Gana, Yoni}, Growth {Tara, GrahaMaitri}). An axis below 5 shows "Growth area · <axis>".
12. **Manglik.** Use the existing `MANGLIK_LAGNA/MOON/VENUS` rules. k = the number of rules that fire (LAGNA is skipped when the lagna is unknown). Level 0 if k = 0, 1 if k = 1, 2 if k ≥ 2 (VERIFY: the partial/full definition). Pair note: both ≥1, or both 0, gives `manglik.pair.balanced`; otherwise `manglik.pair.consult_note` (calm: "many traditions apply cancellations; a family pandit can advise"). The UI names the variant `BASE_NO_CANCELLATION`. The cancellation list is VERIFY and not applied. No fear words, no remedies.
13. **Bond Code v1** (bytes, then base64url without padding). b0 = 0x01; b1 = mn; b2 = (mp−1)<<6 | mars<<4 | hasLagna<<3 | vs; b3 = mr<<4 | (lg or 0xF); b4 = ven<<4 | sun; b5 = L (0..36, UTF-8 byte length of the NFC name, 1..12 code points); then the name; the last byte is CRC-8 (poly 0x07, init 0x00, no reflection, xorout 0x00; check("123456789") = 0xF4) over all prior bytes. Reject the code if any of these hold: input > 128 chars or contains characters outside [A-Za-z0-9_-]; non-canonical trailing bits; decoded length ≠ 7 + L; version ≠ 1; any range is broken; mr ≠ (mn·4 + mp − 1) div 9; hasLagna = 0 but lg ≠ 0xF; vs ≠ the class of mr when mr ∉ {8,9}; a name contains control or bidi characters; the CRC does not match. The link form is `https://<share_host>/b#<code>`; any other host or path is rejected. g, y and nd are recomputed from mn. `BondCodeException` messages never contain input bytes.
14. **Privacy.** Only the user's **own** (self) profile can produce a code. The share screen previews every field and has a toggle for the lagna (default off). Under 18 (age from the self profile) forces initials. Manual or family profiles cannot be shared and never appear on cards: a bond made from one stores `profileRef` with traits computed locally, and its card share is disabled. Copying sets `EXTRA_IS_SENSITIVE`. A deep link opens a preview with "Add bond?"; it never saves, deletes or exports without a tap. The UI says "Deleting removes it from this phone only."
15. **Cards.** 1080×1920 and 1080×1080, drawn on a Canvas, written as PNG to `cacheDir/share/` with a random file name and exposed through FileProvider with a temporary read grant. The folder is wiped on start. The card shows names or initials, score, label, axes, wordmark, `share_host` and "Installed? tap to match". It never shows the code, dates, times or places. Order of intents: WhatsApp `setPackage` → w4b → chooser. The footer "Traditional interpretation, not a scientific prediction." appears on the result, identity, match screens and cards.

## Do not
- Send traits, codes or names to any server, log, analytics, Crashlytics, URL query, file name or notification. Do not use a referrer, `ref=` or Firebase Dynamic Links.
- Add ads, interstitials or rewarded flows (M15), AI interpretation (M13+, opt-in), PDFs (M14), backend or Workers code, or accounts. Do not create the domain, the Facebook App ID or the signing SHA (owner only).
- Use Swiss Ephemeris, GPL/AGPL code, `security-crypto`, or a new QR or CBOR dependency. Do not copy text, art or tables from apps or sites. Write the tables as original data and keep them VERIFY.
- Use fear language ("dosha danger", "bad match"), sell remedies, use pass/fail verdicts, or apply unverified cancellations to scores.
- Hard-code user-facing strings: they go in resources or content packs. Do not loosen golden tolerances or edit fixtures to make a test pass.
